F3nix Browser: Video & Subs

Package ID: com.f3nix.browser  ·  Effective Date: November 24, 2026  ·  Developer: FenixGames

1. Overview & Local-First Design

F3nix Browser: Video & Subs ("the App") is developed by FenixGames. We respect your privacy. The App does not require account registration, does not operate proprietary tracking servers, and stores your browsing history, bookmarks, tabs, downloaded media files, and subtitle projects locally on your device.

2. Data Processed Locally on Your Device

  • Web browsing data: Visited URLs, cookies, cache, and bookmarks are managed locally by Android WebView. You can clear all browsing data at any time from Settings → Clear browsing data, or use Incognito tabs.
  • Media & subtitle processing: Video playback, subtitle translation (via Google ML Kit on-device models), and subtitle hardsub burning (via AndroidX Media3) are performed locally on your phone. Your videos and subtitle files are never uploaded to FenixGames servers.
  • Gallery exports: When enabled, exported files are saved to your device's standard Movies/F3nixBrowser or Music/F3nixBrowser folder using Android Scoped Storage (MediaStore).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob) & Consent (Google UMP)

To keep the App free, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob exclusively on native app utility screens (Downloads, Subtitle Studio, App Open, and Player exit) and never overlaid on third-party web pages.

Google AdMob and its certified ad technology partners may collect and process certain device information to serve, personalize (where consented), cap frequency, prevent fraud, and measure ads, including:

  • Android Advertising ID (AAID) or other device/app identifiers;
  • Approximate location (inferred from IP address);
  • App interactions, diagnostics, and ad performance metrics.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain GDPR consent before loading ads. You can review or withdraw your ad consent at any time via Settings → Ad privacy & consent options, or reset/limit your Advertising ID in your Android device settings.

Learn how Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. On-Device Offline Translation (Google ML Kit)

When you choose to download an offline translation language pack, Google ML Kit downloads the language model directly to your device. Once downloaded, subtitle translation runs completely offline without sending your subtitle text to external servers. You can delete downloaded language packs anytime in Settings.

5. App Permissions

  • Internet & Network State: Required for web browsing, downloading authorized media/language packs, and loading ads.
  • Notifications: Used strictly to show progress while you download authorized media or export subtitled videos.

6. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through third-party services, please contact us so we can assist.

7. Responsible Use & Third-Party Websites

When you visit external websites through the built-in browser, those websites are governed by their own privacy policies and terms of service. F3nix Browser is intended strictly for personal, open-license, public-domain, or authorized content.

8. Data Deletion & Contact

Because FenixGames does not collect or store your personal account data on remote servers, you can delete all local app data instantly via Settings → Clear browsing data or by uninstalling the App from your device.
