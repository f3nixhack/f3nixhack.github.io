Package ID: com.fenixgames.thrusterfit  ·  Effective Date: November 26, 2026  ·  Developer: FenixGames

Medical & Health Disclaimer / Aviso Médico: Thruster Fit is a fitness and general wellness tracking application and is NOT a medical device. Body composition estimates (body fat %, muscle mass, visceral fat, bone mass, metabolic age, BMR, fluid retention, sweat loss, and AI Coach Thruster guidance) are for informational and fitness tracking purposes only and do not substitute professional medical diagnosis, treatment, or advice. Do not use bioimpedance scales if you have a pacemaker or other implanted electronic medical device.

1. Overview & Local-First Storage

Thruster Fit: Balanza y Coach ("the App") is developed by FenixGames. The App does not require user account registration, does not sell your personal or health data, and stores your profiles, weigh-in history, tape measurements, daily water logs, and progress photos locally on your device.

2. Permissions & On-Device Processing

  • Bluetooth (BLE) & Location: Used exclusively to scan, connect, and receive weight and bioimpedance readings from compatible Bluetooth smart scales (Xiaomi, OKOK, Chipsea, Yolanda, Renpho, Eufy, Yunmai, Standard GATT). Location is never stored or transmitted by FenixGames.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition: Used solely when you actively start a voice call with AI Coach Thruster to convert your speech into commands/questions.
  • Camera & Photo Library: Used solely if you choose to save private Before & After Progress Photos inside your device's local app storage.
  • Notifications: Used solely to deliver optional local daily weigh-in and hydration reminders configured by you.
  • Google Health Connect (Optional): Only reads and writes Weight and Body Fat Percentage if you explicitly enable Health Connect sync in Settings. The use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Optional Live AI (Google Gemini)

By default, AI Coach Thruster runs using an on-device local engine with zero external data transfer. Only if you voluntarily enter your own Google AI Studio API key in the voice call settings, your voice prompt and body metrics summary are sent directly to Google's Gemini API to generate real-time coaching responses.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob), GDPR/UMP & In-App Purchases

To keep Thruster Fit free, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob (banners, medium rectangles, interstitials, and optional rewarded videos). Google AdMob and its certified ad partners may collect device identifiers (Android Advertising ID), approximate IP-based location, and ad interaction diagnostics to serve, personalize (where consented), and measure ads.

For users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, the App integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain GDPR consent. You can manage your ad privacy choices anytime under Settings → Privacy & Medical Disclaimer → Ad Privacy Options (GDPR), or remove all ads permanently via Google Play In-App Purchase (thruster_fit_remove_ads).

Learn how Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Children's Privacy

Thruster Fit is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Data Control, Backup, Deletion & Contact

Because FenixGames does not store your health records on remote servers, you have full control on your device: you can export/restore a local JSON backup in Settings, delete individual profiles or progress photos anytime, or uninstall the App to permanently remove all local data.
